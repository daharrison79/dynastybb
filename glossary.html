Basketball Statistics Glossary

This page explains the statistics and analytics used throughout the Dynasty Basketball Analytics Portal.

Traditional Statistics

GP (Games Played)

The number of games a player has appeared in.

PTS / PPG

Total Points and Points Per Game.

PPG = Points ÷ Games

REB / RPG

Total Rebounds and Rebounds Per Game.

RPG = Rebounds ÷ Games

AST / APG

Total Assists and Assists Per Game.

APG = Assists ÷ Games

STL / SPG

Total Steals and Steals Per Game.

BLK / BPG

Total Blocks and Blocks Per Game.

TO / TOPG

Total Turnovers and Turnovers Per Game.

FOUL / FPG

Total Fouls and Fouls Per Game.

Shooting Statistics

FG%

Field Goal Percentage.

FG% = FGM ÷ FGA

3PT%

Three-Point Percentage.

3PT% = 3PM ÷ 3PA

FT%

Free Throw Percentage.

FT% = FTM ÷ FTA

eFG%

Effective Field Goal Percentage. Gives additional credit for made three-point shots.

eFG% = (FGM + (0.5 × 3PM)) ÷ FGA

TS%

True Shooting Percentage. Measures overall scoring efficiency including free throws.

TS% = PTS ÷ (2 × (FGA + (0.44 × FTA)))

Impact Metrics

PF (Points For)

Team points scored while the player, pairing, or lineup was on the floor.

PA (Points Against)

Opponent points scored while the player, pairing, or lineup was on the floor.

POSS

Estimated possessions while the player, pairing, or lineup was on the floor.

+/- (Plus Minus)

Raw scoring differential while on the floor.

PLUS_MINUS = PF − PA

NET RTG (Net Rating)

Measures how many points a team outscores or is outscored by opponents per 100 possessions while a player is on the floor.

((PF − PA) ÷ POSS) × 100
Positive values indicate the team performs better than opponents while the player is on the court.

Efficiency Metrics

EFF (Efficiency Rating)

A box-score metric that rewards positive contributions and penalizes missed shots and turnovers.

EFF = PTS + REB + AST + STL + BLK - (FGA - FGM) - (FTA - FTM) - TO

EFF/G

Average Efficiency Rating per game.

EFF ÷ Games

Lineup Metrics

Best Pairing

Two-player combinations ranked by Net Rating while sharing the floor.

Best Lineup

Five-player lineups ranked by Net Rating while on the court together.

Quick Interpretation Guide

PPG

How much a player scores.

RPG

How much a player rebounds.

APG

How much a player creates offense.

EFF/G

How productive a player is statistically.

NET RTG

How well the team performs while the player is on the floor.

TS%

How efficiently a player scores.
